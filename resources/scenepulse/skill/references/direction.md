# Creative direction

Exeloxylade wants a reusable companion to Bang Story that carries the same creative intention between agents. The first sound vocabulary is deliberately small. Prefer reusable transitions and simple accents over reconstructing environmental action from narration.

Narration leads. Do not rewrite it, regenerate speech, insert pauses, time-stretch it, or make timing decisions from an estimated script length. Use measured video/scene timing. Keep existing music and captions; do not add a second automatic music track.

Style selection changes editorial energy, spacing, camera amplitude, SFX level ceilings, and graphic colors. It does not rewrite the underlying story or illustration style. An educational stickman video can use minimal, playful, or documentary editing.

## Useful choices

- Major scene change: consider a short swish; most ordinary cuts remain silent.
- A graphic appears: consider a pop. Avoid a pop on every subtitle word.
- A visible list advances: consider a click or tick, with spacing.
- One important detail: consider a brief fading box or underline. Position only from visible evidence or explicit coordinates.
- Still composition needing gentle movement: a small push-in or pan. Avoid cutting off faces, captions, or important text.
- Already animated footage: keep camera static by default. Graphic overlays may still be appropriate.
- Calm explanation: often no sound and no graphic.

## Examples

Good: one swish at a chapter transition, a subtle box around a visible map region, then several quiet explanatory scenes.

Overdone: sound on every scene boundary, alternating swishes continuously, camera shake under subtitles, a highlight inferred from narration without knowing the object's location.

## Learning from feedback

Save concrete accepted/rejected examples with the project style and user reason. Update the matching preset or rule after repeated evidence. Do not make one rejected effect a global ban or turn one video style into the universal default. This improves the skill's guidance; it does not retrain model weights.
